# Getting started

## First launch

1. Open **Wallpaper** and pick a shader, an image or a video.
2. Switch on **Show on desktop**. The wallpaper appears behind your icons.
3. Open **Music**, click **Add music** and choose some files. Press play.
4. Open **Widgets**, add a clock and drag it where you like.

Closing the window sends Deskforge to the tray, so your wallpaper and music keep running. Right click the tray icon to play, pause, skip or quit.

## Profiles

A profile stores a wallpaper, a set of widgets and the visualizer settings. Switch profiles from the sidebar. Export one from the **Profiles** page to share it as a `.deskforge` file.

Your music library, accent color and plugin settings are shared by all profiles.

## Good to know

- Widgets on the desktop are display-only: Windows sends mouse clicks to your icons, not to the wallpaper.
- If a video wallpaper stutters, try a lower resolution or the **Battery saver** option.
- Data lives in the app's local storage. Use **Settings > Backup** to export everything.
